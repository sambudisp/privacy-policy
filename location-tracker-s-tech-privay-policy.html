Privacy Policy – Location Tracker - S Tech

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app Location Tracker - S Tech (package name: com.sambudi.offlinegpsloc), published on Google Play by Sambudi Sugya Putra (the "Developer", "we", "us"). It explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored.

1. Summary

  • The app records your device location only while you have started a tracking session.
  • Location may be collected in the background (when the app is closed or not in use) while a tracking session is active.
  • All location data is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers and is not sold or shared with anyone.
  • The app does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, or other personal identifiers.

2. Data the app accesses and why

3. Permissions used

  • Location (fine / coarse) – to get your current position when tracking.
  • Background location – to keep recording your location when the app is closed or not in use while tracking is active. Before this permission is requested, the app shows an in-app disclosure and asks for your consent.
  • Foreground service / notifications – to show a persistent notification while tracking runs, so you always know when your location is being recorded.
  • Run at startup – to resume an ongoing tracking session after the device restarts.

4. Sharing of data

We do not sell, transfer, or share your location data or any other personal data with third parties. Location data never leaves your device through the app.

The app uses the following third-party components provided by Google:

  • Google Maps SDK for Android – to display map tiles for your route history. Google may receive technical data (such as IP address and the map area being displayed) to serve map tiles. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google) – used for app infrastructure. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

5. Data retention and deletion

Location history remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete a single session or all history at any time from within the app (Location History → delete / Delete All). Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device. Because we do not store your data on any server, we hold no copy of it.

6. How to stop location collection

  • Tap Stop Track in the app to end the tracking session.
  • Revoke the location permission at any time in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Location Tracker - S Tech → Permissions).

7. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access under normal Android security rules.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
